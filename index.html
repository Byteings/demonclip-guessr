RULES

How it works.

01

Watch a random clip

A random level and a random 10–30 second slice of its verification video start automatically. Click the clip or press R to replay it as often as you want.

02

Guess under the clip

When the clip ends, type the level name and press Enter. There are no video links, thumbnails or name lists.

03

One hint every 30 seconds

No hint is shown at the start. Hints arrive automatically; guessing earlier scores more.

04

Multiplayer

Pass & play supports up to six custom-named players on one device. Online rooms let everyone join from an invite link in their own browser.

Scoring & controls

  • +100 base points, multiplied up to 2× for top-ranked levels.
  • +speed bonus up to 50 points, declining after the clip ends.
  • +streak bonus of 10 per correct answer, capped at 100.
  • −20 for a wrong answer. Skips score zero and break the streak.
